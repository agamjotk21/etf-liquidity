Analyzed trading costs for 34 ETFs (2018-2026) by estimating bid-ask spreads from daily price data (Corwin-Schultz, Abdi-Ranaldo); found that the estimates are driven by volatility rather than liquidity (R² = 0.81 on volatility alone; SPY estimated at 23.7 bp vs. a 0.24 bp tick floor) and that stress-period widening tracks volatility (COVID bond-ETF spreads 3.5x vs. volatility 5.1x), and recommended volatility-adjusted spread screens plus quote-level data for any ETF liquidity pricing decision.
